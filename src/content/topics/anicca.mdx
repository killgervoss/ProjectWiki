## What does it mean?

Anicca is commonly translated as **impermanence**. In Buddhist teachings, it describes how conditioned things arise, change, and cease.

## An everyday example

A feeling can become stronger, weaken, and pass. Observing that change offers an accessible starting point for understanding the concept.

You can also explore [dependent origination](/topics/dependent-origination/), which concerns the conditions through which phenomena arise.
