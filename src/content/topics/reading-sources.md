## Follow the citation

A useful knowledge article helps readers understand an idea and locate the passage on which the explanation is based.

## Preserve differences

Name the author, tradition, or historical context when it changes the interpretation. Keep quotations distinct from your own explanations.

## Keep articles connected

Use related-topic links to offer the next step without requiring readers to follow a fixed sequence.
